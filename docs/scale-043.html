C Harmonic Minor

3rd Position, two octaves
Spiccato, eighth notesTempo goal ♩ = 72–112
TODAY'S FOCUS

Let the bow drop and bounce from the air in a shallow smile shape. Use the natural bounce of the stick near the balance point, not your fingers. In 3rd position the heel of your hand lightly touches the violin body. Use that contact as your guide for every shift into 3rd.

The scale

Written bowing, every finger number shown.

Right hand first

Play the bowing alone on the open G string until the stroke feels easy and the sound is even. Then play the scale.
