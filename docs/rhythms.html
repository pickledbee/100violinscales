Rhythm Variations

Apply any of these rhythms to any scale on the site. Rhythms make the fingers move at different speeds, which exposes weak spots and makes even scales faster and cleaner.

Long-short

Great for finger speed: the short note forces the next finger to drop fast.

Short-long

The reverse. Snap the finger down on the short note, relax on the long one.

Gallop

One eighth and two sixteenths. Keep the sixteenths exactly even.

Reverse gallop

Two sixteenths and an eighth. Land precisely on the beat.

Triplets

Three even notes per beat. Count "trip-o-let" out loud at first.

Sixteenths

Four even notes per beat with a steady metronome click.

Quarter and two eighths

A long note to listen for pitch, then two quick ones.

Syncopation

Short, long, short. Keep the bow even on the long note.
