31

B Harmonic Minor

2nd Position, two octaves
Louré (portato), 4 per bowTempo goal ♩ = 50–72
TODAY'S FOCUS

Four gentle pulses in each bow. Keep the bow moving the whole time; shape each note with a slight swell of weight. In 2nd position your 1st finger sits where your 2nd finger used to be in 1st position. Keep the thumb opposite the 1st or 2nd finger.

The scale

Written bowing, every finger number shown.

Right hand first

Play the bowing alone on the open G string until the stroke feels easy and the sound is even. Then play the scale.

Matching arpeggio

Same key, same 2nd position. Slur three.
