TODAY'S FOCUS

The dotted eighth and the sixteenth share one bow with a small stop before the short note. Keep the rhythm exact: the sixteenth is short and late. In 3rd position the heel of your hand lightly touches the violin body. Use that contact as your guide for every shift into 3rd.
