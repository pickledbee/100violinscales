TODAY'S FOCUS

Short notes with the bow staying on the string. Stop the bow after each note and let the string ring for a tiny moment of silence. In 3rd position the heel of your hand lightly touches the violin body. Use that contact as your guide for every shift into 3rd.

The scale

Written bowing, every finger number shown.

Right hand first

Play the bowing alone on the open G string until the stroke feels easy and the sound is even. Then play the scale.
