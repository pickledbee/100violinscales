B Harmonic Minor

2nd Position, two octaves
Slurred staccato, 4 per bowTempo goal ♩ = 40–66
TODAY'S FOCUS

Four little stopped strokes in one bow. Start slow and keep every stroke the same size; this builds toward up-bow staccato. In 2nd position your 1st finger sits where your 2nd finger used to be in 1st position. Keep the thumb opposite the 1st or 2nd finger.
