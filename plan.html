A 20-Minute Scale Routine

20:00
The timer moves to the next step automatically.
  1. 3 min · Open strings with whole bows. Listen for one clean, ringing tone on each string.
  2. 5 min · Today’s scale with separate bows, slowly. Fix every note that does not ring.
  3. 7 min · The written bowing with the metronome ladder. Two clean runs, then four clicks faster.
  4. 3 min · The matching arpeggio.
  5. 2 min · One rhythm variation, then play the scale once more as music, not as an exercise.
