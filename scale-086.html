86

E Major on the E String

One octave on one string: 1st, 3rd and 5th positions
Slur 7Tempo goal ♩ = 50–80
TODAY'S FOCUS

Seven notes per bow means the bow change lands in a new place every time. Count carefully and let the bow change be invisible. Stay on one string the whole way. Slide on the finger you are leaving and lighten it during each shift.

The scale

Written bowing, every finger number shown.

Right hand first

Play the bowing alone on the open E string until the stroke feels easy and the sound is even. Then play the scale.

Slow-motion pass

Separate bows, every finger shown. Listen to each shift.
