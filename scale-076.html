76

F Major

5th Position, two octaves
Détaché, eighth notesTempo goal ♩ = 60–96
TODAY'S FOCUS

Middle of the bow, about a third of the bow per note. Let the arm lead from the elbow so the string crossings are smooth, not lifted. In 5th position the hand starts to come around the shoulder of the violin. Bring the thumb partly under the neck and keep the elbow forward.

The scale

Written bowing, every finger number shown.

Right hand first

Play the bowing alone on the open G string until the stroke feels easy and the sound is even. Then play the scale.

Matching arpeggio

Same key, same 5th position. Slur three.
