About the author

Bruce Bowers

Bruce Bowers has played the violin for 47 years and has taught for more than 20. He is a registered Suzuki Method teacher through Book 6, and he has performed as concertmaster, assistant concertmaster and principal second violin in local symphony orchestras.

He teaches Suzuki violin in the Pulaski and Green Bay area of Wisconsin, where he works with students of every age and stage, from first lessons to advanced repertoire.

This site brings together the scale and bowing work an intermediate player needs in one place: every position from 1st to 5th, every core bow stroke, and every common slur pattern, free for anyone to use.
